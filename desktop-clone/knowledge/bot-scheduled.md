# Your Bot and Scheduled tasks

Your Bot is a persistent personal assistant with its own conversation, identity, appearance and behavior. Use its setup flow, then its name/avatar editor or Settings → Bot to manage it. Appearance edits include the name and animated avatar. Behavior includes memory, initiative and check frequency. Some saved changes wait for an active reply to finish.

The mobile companion also offers your personal Bot in its sidebar when the device has access. It uses the same conversation, animated custom avatar, message bubbles and reactions. Tap its mobile header for status, watch information and Snooze/Wake up. Desktop remains the runtime and must be open and connected. Bot appearance, permissions and individual watch editing remain on desktop; private internal watch chats are not exposed to the companion.

## Conversation and browser

A successfully loaded browser or other host tool group can carry forward into the next foreground reply. Available actions are checked against current access settings; earlier plugin grants are not replayed. The browser card highlights its background on hover without showing a border.

In a narrow conversation pane, the name and avatar float over the messages with a soft shadow, leaving the conversation its full height. They remain visible in an empty chat. In a wide pane, a right-side Bot card shows its identity, Message and Edit bot controls, its browser, watches, recent tool activity and saved outputs when available. Opening the workspace returns the identity to the floating layout so the conversation keeps room. Recent activity links return to the relevant message in this Bot conversation; they are not separate delegated Work chats. Message focuses the composer in TinyGPT; phone, SMS, calls and Slack are not Bot contact channels in this interface.

Enable Browse the web in Bot access settings to allow browser/research tools, subject to shared permissions and approval checks. Foreground Bot turns can load the interactive browser group, including listing/reusing tabs, snapshots, click, type, scroll, hover and key actions. Active browser work has a live preview and Watch control; the browser card uses a browser illustration and opens the same workspace browser when clicked anywhere. Its compact surface highlights on hover, with Ready, Browsing, Off or Sleeping beneath the name. Interactive Bot tabs survive between replies while the app stays open, even if not watched. Quiet research tabs are still temporary. Restarting does not restore open tabs; persistent browser-profile cookies can keep a site signed in. Login and saved-password entry remain user-controlled. Private proactive checks have read-only browser access, including screenshots and image inspection, and cannot click through changes. Browser discovery identifies whether this turn is foreground or a proactive read-only check; a missing click action during a watch is an intentional restriction, not evidence that the app lacks interaction tools. Continue in the Bot conversation for interactive changes, subject to the normal permissions.

The Bot acts directly inside its ongoing chat. Private watch checks use their own internal Bot conversations and can deliver useful findings back to the main Bot chat. The Bot does not currently create or delegate user-facing Work chats, run shell commands, or use Work-only file/task tools. Its declared project file tools are separate from Work-only actions. The Bot uses the currently selected inference connection/model and shares Chat's effort setting; it has no independent Bot effort default. These are current capabilities, not a promise that a particular connected plugin exposes a needed action.

If a Bot message cannot finish, earlier delivered messages stay saved. The error notice can distinguish authentication, provider limits, temporary provider failures and a rejected conversation format. A repeating conversation-format error can be reported through Diagnostics; the diagnostic records a fixed category and HTTP status without copying the upstream error body. A stopped reply does not establish whether a requested account change succeeded: inspect the recorded tool results and saved messages before continuing.

## Snooze and wake

Choose Snooze in the wide Bot card or Snooze bot in the sidebar Bot menu. The sidebar and conversation avatars use the snooze animation and the status reads Sleeping. Snooze stops current Bot replies and private checks, blocks new Bot replies and pauses proactive checks and delivery until you explicitly wake the Bot. The conversation shows Wake bot in place of the composer; the card and sidebar menu also offer Wake. Sleep persists after restarting TinyGPT. Memory, permissions and individual watch pause settings are preserved. Your separate user-created Scheduled tasks continue normally. Due Bot watches can resume after waking; sleep does not run missed checks in a batch.

Keeping an eye on uses compact single-line watch rows with a clock icon with a three-dot menu with Pause (or Resume), Check now and Remove. Check now runs a private, read-only check immediately without changing the next scheduled check or resuming a paused watch. It is unavailable while the Bot is sleeping or already checking; if another task is using the runtime, wait for it to finish. Next-check timing appears in a styled tooltip when you hover or keyboard-focus the row, and at the top of its menu, rather than as a second line. Paused watches retain a small Paused label. Recent activity groups tool steps into up to five recent requests or proactive updates. Each row uses the original request or update text, links to its reply, and keeps a representative tool icon. Its tooltip shows reply status and the number of tool steps. These are display labels from existing messages, not another model-generated summary or evidence that a task succeeded. New requests enter at the top; additional steps update that request instead of filling the list. Motion preferences are respected. Hover or keyboard focus reveals the three-dot menu to open the reply and, for browser activity, the browser. These controls do not erase tool history.

## Manage Bot access

The Bot's plugin access controls refer to permission to use accounts in that conversation, not simply whether a plugin is installed. Turning access off prevents subsequent use and removes its grants; it cannot retract an action already dispatched. Account connections can remain available to other conversations. Newly selected plugin tools may become callable on the next message.

Bot memory and personal-memory sharing are distinct controls. Bot notes supply persistent personality, preferences and remembered context according to its configuration. Do not assume pausing a personal memory deletes Bot notes or original messages. Inspect Bot configuration when asking what it remembers.

In Settings → Bot, Remember useful details controls Bot memory; Browse the web controls browser/research use. Take initiative enables proactive work between 8 AM and 10 PM; Check in periodically offers Off, Every 30 minutes and Every hour. Save changes applies the access/behavior edits. Under Personalization, choose Personality, About you, Instructions or Notes, edit its text and select Save personalization. Treat these notes as private context; do not store credentials there.

## Proactive watches

Saving a watch schedules future checks; it does not perform the action described in its prompt. Repeated unchanged watch or memory saves temporarily remove that maintenance tool from the current reply so the Bot can return to the actual task. It becomes available again on the next reply. Productive writes and repeated observations are not a task-length limit. An identical watch prompt and interval reuse the saved watch even if a different title is proposed, and title matching ignores capitalization. Existing watches and user pauses are preserved.

When enabled, the Bot can perform private checks and maintain watches for interests or commitments. These watches are separate from the user-facing Scheduled list. Checks should report worthwhile developments rather than repeated generic messages. Autonomous checks cannot wait on an approval card and have restrictions on external messaging, purchases, edits and commands. Missing permission can prevent a watch from doing useful work.

Interest watches check at intervals of at least 30 minutes; they are not 5-minute reminders. Saving an identical watch does not reset its next check or resume a watch you paused. The general Personal check-in is managed by the user's check-frequency setting, not the Bot's interest-watch tool. The wide Bot card lets you pause or resume individual watches; initiative must also be on for checks to run.

Ask the Bot to stop watching a topic or to remove verification watches after the task is done. Its bot_watch_remove tool removes a specific Bot interest watch by the saved ID, including paused watches, and remains available in the conversation when initiative is off. A private proactive check may remove only its own interest watch after verifying its completion condition. It cannot remove a user-created Scheduled task, another watch during a private check, or the general Personal check-in. Successful removal stops future checks and updates Keeping an eye on; it preserves past conversations and run history. Retrying a removed ID reports that the watch is already absent. The Bot must check the tool result before claiming removal. Watch minutes are the recurring check interval, not an automatic expiry: a 30-minute watch does not disappear after 30 minutes.

## Schedule your own task

Open Scheduled, create a task and enter its prompt. Choose Once (date/time), Hourly, Daily, Weekdays or Weekly; Weekly also selects a day. The editor shows the timezone used for times. Advanced contains Name, Start each run in a new chat and Mode (Chat or Work). Tasks use the current model and selected plugins. Inspect the next run and run history; available task actions include running now, stopping, pausing/resuming and removing. Generated starter ideas are suggestions, not enabled tasks until saved. Scheduled work requires the desktop application and relevant connections to be available; do not assume a closed or sleeping computer runs jobs. Remote keep-awake prevents automatic sleep only while its configured conditions apply.
